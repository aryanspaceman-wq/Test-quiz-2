BEYOND THE BUZZ

What really drives teenage energy drink consumption?

“Your age. Your habits. Your environment. Your feed. What influences the choices teenagers make?”

We're investigating energy drink consumption among teenagers in Qatar to understand the role of social influence, digital culture, everyday routines, and the local environment.

TAKE THE QUIZWHY THIS STUDY MATTERS

What we're investigating

Seven lenses on one question

We study the factors linked to teenage energy drink consumption, separately and together, to understand associations, not to assign blame or predict individuals.

Age & location

How age groups and broad areas of Qatar relate to consumption.

Shops & environment

Proximity to places that sell energy drinks and perceived availability.

Peers & word-of-mouth

Friends' recommendations and social-circle norms, without assuming pressure.

Social media & trends

Exposure to influencers, ads, and online culture across platforms.

Gaming, study & sleep

Routines, examinations, tiredness, and the drive to stay alert.

Motivations & preferences

Everyday motivations, measured as behaviour, not fixed personality types.

Caffeine & health awareness

Familiarity with caffeine, sugar, and potential health effects.
